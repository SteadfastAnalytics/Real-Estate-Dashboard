# Real Estate — Leads and Regions Dashboard

![Real Estate Leads and Regions Dashboard](images/real-estate-dashboard.png)

## The Business

A real estate team generating leads from multiple sources — open houses, referrals, social media, past clients, Zillow — needed to know which of those sources were actually worth the money, not just which ones generated the most volume.

## What's On It

- **Lead economics** — total lead spend, total leads, and cost per lead, filterable by individual agent
- **Closed deals by lead source** — so it's immediately clear which channels convert, not just which ones generate inquiries
- **Commission by region** — East Side, North Valley, Central, and West End, compared side by side
- **Days on market trend** — tracking how quickly listings are actually moving over time

## The Question It Answers

*Which lead sources are actually worth paying for — and is my pipeline speeding up or slowing down?*

Instead of guessing whether that ad spend on Zillow is worth it, the agent can see closed deals by source directly next to commission by region, and make a real decision with real numbers behind it.

## Built With

Power BI, connected directly to the team's CRM and lead-tracking data — refreshed automatically, no manual exports.
